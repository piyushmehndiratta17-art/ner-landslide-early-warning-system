#### 2. Column 1: The Crisis & IoT Architecture
- **Text Block:** The North Eastern Region of India (NER) accounts for over 60% of recurring landslide hazards nationally. Torrential monsoons, Zone V seismicity, and weathered shale lithology routinely paralyze lifeline highways (NH-10, NH-6, NH-29) and railway tracks (Lumding-Badarpur, Tupul).
- **Key Hardware Array:**
  - *Tipping Bucket Rain Gauge* (Rainfall accumulation & intensity in mm/h)
  - *Vibrating Wire Piezometer* (Pore water pressure in kPa)
  - *Campbell TDR Soil Moisture Sensor* (Saturation %)
  - *In-Place MEMS Inclinometer & Geophone* (Shear displacement velocity & seismic PGA)
- **Edge Telemetry:** Solar-harvested ESP32 microcontrollers communicating over LoRaWAN (865 MHz) with 10–15 km valley transmission range.

#### 3. Column 2: Physics-Informed Machine Learning Core
- **Why Physics-Informed?** Conventional deep learning treats slopes as black boxes, leading to false alarm fatigue during heavy monsoons. Our system grounds machine learning in Terzaghi's effective stress principle:
  $$\tau_f = c' + (\sigma_n - u_w) \tan \phi'$$
- **Eastern Himalayan Rainfall Intensity-Duration Threshold:**
  $$I = 14.82 \cdot D^{-0.39}$$
  *(Calibrated against Geological Survey of India empirical records).*
- **Saito Inverse Velocity ($\frac{1}{v} \to 0$) Time-to-Failure Forecasting:**
  Tracks the reciprocal of displacement velocity to project the exact collapse window in hours during tertiary creep.
- **Drone Vision Fissure Extraction:**
  High-pass directional filtering highlights surface tension cracks and scarp fractures on drone orthomosaics, fused with IoT data.

#### 4. Column 3: Early Warning System & Disaster Management Impact
- **NDMA SACHET Compatible (CAP v1.2):** Standardized XML/JSON payloads with geofenced alert circles, severity ratings, and automated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) actions.
- **Multilingual Emergency Broadcasting:** Real-time SMS and WhatsApp dispatches in English, Hindi, Assamese (অসমীয়া), Bengali (বাংলা), and Mizo.
- **Four Alert Tiers:**
  - 🟢 Green: Normal baseline ($LSI < 0.35$)
  - 🟡 Yellow: Advisory ($0.35 \le LSI < 0.60$)
  - 🟠 Orange: High Alert ($0.60 \le LSI < 0.80$)
  - 🔴 Red: Critical Evacuation ($LSI \ge 0.80$)
- **Cost-to-Benefit Ratio:** Corridor deployment costs ~₹8–12 Lakhs vs. single-day road blockage loss of >₹2 Crores.
